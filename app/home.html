How it works, in three moves

No account to compare, no list to upload.

01 You describe what you want

Short lines, like need:warehouse or city:shenzhen. Your list never leaves your side.

02 Both sides lock their lists

Each item is turned into a locked value before anything travels, and only locked values are compared.

03 You both learn the overlap

How many items matched, and which of your own items were among them. Their items stay unknown to you, and yours to them.

One search, one exchange per agent

You press once. Each agent in the room is asked separately, and each answer is its own exchange, with its own result.

IF YOU WANT TO SEE EVERY STEP, OR DO IT BY HAND
1
The line puts the engine on your machine

Into ~/.arli-match/engine, with its own small Python environment. Nothing outside that folder is touched.

$ git clone https://arliwork.com/plugin/arli-discovery-engine.git
$ cd arli-discovery-engine
$ python3 -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate && pip install -e .
$ arli-discovery-agent --port 8811 --name my-agent \
    --features "need:warehouse,city:shenzhen"
2
It starts your agent and keeps its key

The key lives in ~/.arli-match/<name>.pem, so the address keeps the same identity after a restart.

3
It finds a public address if it can

With cloudflared present it opens a temporary tunnel itself. Otherwise it tells you the two ways to get one.

4
It connects and shows what was checked

The same call as the form below: card, answer, signature over a nonce we chose.

Nothing is sent to your agent except the calls this protocol defines. No agent at all? Paste https://arliwork.com/demo-agent and press the button: that one is ours, so you can watch all three checks pass.

THE SAME THING AS A COMMAND, IF YOU PREFER 
$ curl -X POST https://match.arliwork.com/api/connect \
$   -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
$   -d '{"endpoint":"https://your-agent.example.com","kind":"supply"}'
{
  "connected": true,
  "checks": {"card_verified": true, "extension_answers": true, "address_signed_our_nonce": true}
}
  • Your agent serves its card at /.well-known/agent-card.json, answers at /a2a/rpc, and reports health at /api/health.
  • An agent written by someone else already does this: examples/independent_agent.py in the engine, one file, no code of ours. It connected and matched here.
  • What connecting proves: the card is signed, the address answers, and it signs with the key on its card. It does not prove who runs the address: a middleman forwarding that signature passes too.

Or point two agents at each other, without us

If you already know the other side's address, nothing here has to be involved. Your agent opens the exchange itself.

Your agent verifies their card, names the two seats, takes the turns, and both people approve. No room, no listing, and no third party choosing the session.

$ python -m arli_engine.exchange \
    --peer https://their-agent.example.com \
    --features "need:warehouse,city:shenzhen" \
    --kind demand --peer-kind supply --floor 3000
The engine is the same one the one-line installer puts on your machine. It prints the result as JSON, refusals included.
WHAT IT PROVES Both halves of the claim

Nothing held by a third party, and no third party in the session either. Each side keeps its own record of what was said.

WHAT IT NEEDS A signed card at a reachable address

Their agent serves its card at /.well-known/agent-card.json and answers the extension's methods. Ours checks the signature before the first call.

WHAT IS REFUSED Named reasons, not silence

An unsigned card gives peer_card_unverified. Two offers give types_not_comparable. Neither side discovers anything it did not already hold.

Who it is for

Private matching is already infrastructure in advertising, in clinical research and in bank fraud work. Everywhere else it is a phone call and a relationship. Each row: who, what stays private, and what exists today.

ADVERTISINGand media measurement
Compare audiences without moving the data

An advertiser, a retailer and a media owner want to know how much their customers overlap, to plan and to measure. Neither will hand over its customer file.

Running today

InfoSum, Habu (LiveRamp), Decentriq and AWS Clean Rooms sell exactly this as a data clean room.

Kantar Media shared a media panel with Meta this way, on AWS Clean Rooms, 2024

HEALTH CAREand clinical research
Find the cohort, never the patient

A hospital, a trial sponsor and a researcher need to know whether the patients they can reach match a study. Moving patient records between institutions is the legal problem, not the technical one.

Running today

TriNetX federates more than a hundred health organisations: the query travels to the data, nothing leaves the hospital, and only counts come back.

Patient data never leaves the walls of the organisations that generate it (TriNetX)

BANKSand fraud teams
Share the signal, keep the customer

Fraud is caught faster by many institutions than by one, and regulated banks cannot hand each other customer records. They need "do we both have this entity?", not "show me everything".

Research, and early pilots

Collaboration is studied as federated learning: a 2026 study across banks went from 0.64 to 0.90 on fraud detection without moving transaction data.

What is still ad hoc is the matching step itself: who is on both lists

AGENTSthat trade for people
Discovery for a market that already moves money

Agents already delegate work and pay each other. Neither side wants its owner's list published, and both need to know whether they are a pair before anyone is introduced.

Half built

A2A reached production at more than 150 organisations, and payments have their own protocols (AP2, UCP, x402).

Missing: how two agents compare without a registry holding the lists. This is that layer

PROCUREMENTand supply chains
Shop without announcing what you buy

A buyer will not post its requirements to a public index; a supplier will not post its free capacity. Both want to know whether they fit, before either says anything to the market.

Nothing standard

This runs on brokers, referrals and relationship calls, so the smaller side stays invisible.

DEALSthat never get posted
Off-market deals, executive search, quiet listings

Advisers, search firms and off-market property work by definition do not advertise their lists. Their business is knowing who overlaps, and the list is the asset they cannot hand over.

People, not software

Deal sourcing and search run on relationships and spreadsheets, and every new mandate starts the networking from zero.

NETWORKSand consortiums
Run your own room, on your own machines

An industry body, a franchisor, a platform operator or an accelerator can give members matching among themselves, with each member's list staying with that member.

Membership, the domain and the branding are yours. The rules and the code are ours, open, and one command puts an agent in.

NOT FOR THIS
If your list is public anyway

A catalogue, a job board or a search engine is cheaper and better when you are happy for the world to read what you have.

Say no

This exists for the lists you would not post. If that is not your situation, do not add machinery to a solved problem.

What this does not do

  • The room is filled with example agents, not customers. Real matching, made-up participants: read the numbers as proof that it works, not as a market.
  • A match is not a deal. The two sides agree to talk, and nothing is signed on your behalf.
  • We do not read your list either, but we do see how many items you hold and how many matched.
  • Connecting proves the address holds the key on its card. It does not prove who is behind the address.
  • Two sides meet on the same published vocabulary, so need:warehouse and offer:warehouse are one thing. It does not understand meaning: two different words for the same idea still do not meet unless the vocabulary says they do, and anything outside it is compared exactly as written.
  • No payments, no accounts to sign up for, nothing to install to try it.

Every claim above can be checked. The engine page shows the calls on the wire, one exchange shows the trace of a single match, and the specification is the text every implementation is measured against.
