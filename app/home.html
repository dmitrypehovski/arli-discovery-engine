How it works, in three moves

No account to compare, no list to upload.

01 You describe what you want

Short lines, like need:warehouse or city:shenzhen. Your list never leaves your side.

02 Both sides lock their lists

Each item is turned into a locked value before anything travels, and only locked values are compared.

03 You both learn the overlap

How many items matched, and which of your own items were among them. Their items stay unknown to you, and yours to them.

One search, one exchange per agent

You press once. Each agent in the room is asked separately, and each answer is its own exchange, with its own result.

Connect your own agent

If your agent already runs somewhere and speaks this protocol, give us its address. Your list stays on your machine: we only call the agent at its own address.

1
Start an agent and give it your list

One command. It prints the three addresses it serves.

$ git clone https://arliwork.com/plugin/arli-discovery-engine.git
$ cd arli-discovery-engine
$ python3 -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate && pip install -e .
$ arli-discovery-agent --port 8811 --name my-agent \
    --features "need:warehouse,city:shenzhen"
2
Put it behind a public address

We refuse localhost and private addresses, on purpose, because an address is something a stranger sends us. A tunnel is fine for a test.

$ cloudflared tunnel --url http://127.0.0.1:8811
# it prints a public address, something like random-words.trycloudflare.com
3
Paste that address below

We check three things, and tell you which one failed if one does.

Nothing is ever sent to your agent except the three calls this protocol defines. No agent of your own yet? Paste https://arliwork.com/demo-agent and press the button: that is an agent we host, so you can watch all three checks pass. 
—
THE SAME THING AS A COMMAND, IF YOU PREFER 
$ curl -X POST https://match.arliwork.com/api/connect \
$   -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
$   -d '{"endpoint":"https://your-agent.example.com","kind":"supply"}'
{
  "connected": true,
  "checks": {"card_verified": true, "extension_answers": true, "address_signed_our_nonce": true}
}
  • Your agent has to serve its card at /.well-known/agent-card.json and answer at /a2a/rpc. The specification has the exact shapes.
  • An agent written by someone else already does this: examples/independent_agent.py in the engine home, one file, no code of ours, and it connected and matched here.
  • What connecting proves: the card is signed, the address answers, and it signs with the key on its card. It does not prove who runs the address: a middleman forwarding that signature passes too.

Who it is for

Anywhere the list of who you know is worth more than a public announcement of it.

Studios and agencies

Find work without showing your client book, your rates or your pipeline.

Buyers and operators

Find suppliers without telling the market what you are shopping for.

Funds and platforms

Match portfolio companies and their needs without publishing either side's list.

Anyone two-sided

Two agents with nothing to announce and everything to compare.

Agent builders

Your agent keeps its own list and still takes part. Or write your own agent: the protocol is open.

Networks

Run a room for your own members, on your own machines, with the same rules.

What this does not do

  • The room is filled with example agents, not customers. Real matching, made-up participants: read the numbers as proof that it works, not as a market.
  • A match is not a deal. The two sides agree to talk, and nothing is signed on your behalf.
  • We do not read your list either, but we do see how many items you hold and how many matched.
  • Connecting proves the address holds the key on its card. It does not prove who is behind the address.
  • No payments, no accounts to sign up for, nothing to install to try it.

Every claim above can be checked. The engine page shows the calls on the wire, one exchange shows the trace of a single match, and the specification is the text every implementation is measured against.
