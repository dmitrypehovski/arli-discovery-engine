How it works, in three moves

No account to compare, no list to upload.

01 You describe what you want

Short lines, like need:warehouse or city:shenzhen. Your list never leaves your side.

02 Both sides lock their lists

Each item is turned into a locked value before anything travels, and only locked values are compared.

03 You both learn the overlap

How many items matched, and which of your own items were among them. Their items stay unknown to you, and yours to them.

One search, one exchange per agent

You press once. Each agent in the room is asked separately, and each answer is its own exchange, with its own result.

IF YOU WANT TO SEE EVERY STEP, OR DO IT BY HAND
1
The line puts the engine on your machine

Into ~/.arli-match/engine, with its own small Python environment. Nothing outside that folder is touched.

$ git clone https://arliwork.com/plugin/arli-discovery-engine.git
$ cd arli-discovery-engine
$ python3 -m venv .venv && . .venv/bin/activate && pip install -e .
$ arli-discovery-agent --port 8811 --name my-agent \
    --features "need:warehouse,city:shenzhen"
2
It starts your agent and keeps its key

The key lives in ~/.arli-match/<name>.pem, so the address keeps the same identity after a restart.

3
It finds a public address if it can

With cloudflared present it opens a temporary tunnel itself. Otherwise it tells you the two ways to get one.

4
It connects and shows what was checked

The same call as the form below: card, answer, signature over a nonce we chose.

Nothing is sent to your agent except the calls this protocol defines. No agent at all? Paste https://arliwork.com/demo-agent and press the button: that one is ours, so you can watch all three checks pass.

THE SAME THING AS A COMMAND, IF YOU PREFER 
$ curl -X POST https://match.arliwork.com/api/connect \
$   -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
$   -d '{"endpoint":"https://your-agent.example.com","kind":"supply"}'
{
  "connected": true,
  "checks": {"card_verified": true, "extension_answers": true, "address_signed_our_nonce": true}
}
  • Your agent serves its card at /.well-known/agent-card.json, answers at /a2a/rpc, and reports health at /api/health.
  • An agent written by someone else already does this: examples/independent_agent.py in the engine, one file, no code of ours. It connected and matched here.
  • What connecting proves: the card is signed, the address answers, and it signs with the key on its card. It does not prove who runs the address: a middleman forwarding that signature passes too.

Who it is for

Private matching is not a new idea. It runs the advertising market, it is entering health care and finance, and it is the piece agent commerce is missing. Below: who uses it today, and where it has nowhere to go yet.

ADVERTISING AND COMMERCE MEDIA

Match audiences without moving data

Advertisers, retailers and media owners compare customer overlap to plan and measure campaigns. Today this means a data clean room, a vendor in the middle, and a legal review. An agent with its own list does the same comparison and keeps its own copy.

HEALTH AND LIFE SCIENCES

Find the cohort, not the patient

Hospitals, trial sponsors and researchers need to know whether their cohorts overlap. Moving patient records between institutions is the legal problem, so the comparison has to happen without the records travelling.

FINANCE AND FRAUD CONSORTIA

Share the signal, keep the customer

Banks and payment firms catch fraud faster together than alone, and cannot hand each other customer data. They need to ask "do we both have this?", not "show me yours".

PROCUREMENT AND SUPPLY CHAINS

Shop without announcing what you buy

A buyer will not publish its requirements to a public index, and a supplier will not publish its capacity. Both want to know whether they fit, before either says anything to the market.

DEALS THAT NEVER GET POSTED

Off-market, executive search, pocket listings

M&A advisers, executive search firms and off-market property work by definition do not post their lists. Their business is knowing who overlaps, and the list is the asset they will not hand over.

AGENT BUILDERS AND MARKETPLACES

Discovery for the agents already trading

A2A reached production at 150+ organisations and agent payments have their own protocols. Neither says how two agents learn they are a match. This is that layer: connect by address, compare, and prove what was compared.

NETWORKS AND CONSORTIUMS

Run your own room, on your own machines

Industry bodies, franchisors and platform operators can run a room for their members: same rules, their machines, their members' lists staying with each member.

NOT FOR THIS

If your list is public anyway

A catalogue, a search engine or a job board is cheaper and better when you are happy for everyone to read what you have. This exists for the lists you would not post.

What this does not do

  • The room is filled with example agents, not customers. Real matching, made-up participants: read the numbers as proof that it works, not as a market.
  • A match is not a deal. The two sides agree to talk, and nothing is signed on your behalf.
  • We do not read your list either, but we do see how many items you hold and how many matched.
  • Connecting proves the address holds the key on its card. It does not prove who is behind the address.
  • No payments, no accounts to sign up for, nothing to install to try it.

Every claim above can be checked. The engine page shows the calls on the wire, one exchange shows the trace of a single match, and the specification is the text every implementation is measured against.
