What happens in one scan

Five moves, in this order, every time. Nothing outside them is allowed, and a refusal comes back as a code from a published list rather than as silence.

01You declareWhat you hold, privately, and whether you are buying or offering. This never leaves your side.
02The pair opensOnly if the intents face each other. Two buyers are not a pair and are refused by code, not scored.
03Blind exchangeThe other side publishes a session key and signs values it cannot read. You compare without either list being visible.
04Your floor decidesBelow the overlap you declared, nothing comes back. The match happened; you chose not to see it.
05Both people consentAgents agreeing opens nothing. Each side's person answers separately, and both answers are recorded.

One feature per line, topic:value. You declare whether you are buying or offering, because a pair of two buyers or two sellers is not a pair and the engine refuses it with a code.

Where a private match is worth money

Ten situations where the list is the asset and the introduction is the product. Each one is the same protocol with a different vocabulary.

01 · M&A SCOUTING

Buyers and targets

A buyer will not publish which capabilities it is hunting, and a founder will not announce that it is open to a sale. They learn they overlap on sector, stage and region, and nothing else.

02 · TALENT

Hiring without a public CV dump

Candidates hold their own skill set in their agent; companies hold the role they need. Overlap is found without either side posting a searchable profile.

03 · SUPPLY CHAINS

Buyers and suppliers

Match on capability, region and compliance marks. Prices, volumes and lanes never enter the exchange.

04 · GRANTS

Funded consortia

A programme needs partners with specific capabilities and cannot publish which applicants are short. Overlap is measured, the applicants stay unnamed.

05 · MARKETPLACES

Routing without a catalogue

Send work to the right seller without publishing what every seller can do. The catalogue stays in each seller's agent.

06 · PARTNERING

Two companies before the first call

Answer "is this worth a quarter of someone's time" without either side revealing its portfolio.

07 · HEALTH AND CLINICAL

Trials and sites

A sponsor needs sites with a patient profile; a site will not publish its population. Above a declared floor only, and both sides' principals consent.

08 · FINANCE

Allocating to specialised funds

An allocator scans managers for mandate overlap without either side disclosing the book.

09 · PUBLIC SECTOR

Procurement with sealed bids

Bidders learn whether they are a fit before bidding, without publishing their proposed price or approach.

10 · AGENT ECONOMY

Agents that transact alone

Two agents must decide whether to engage before their principals are awake. The card says who they are, the grammar bounds the talk, the envelopes make it auditable, and consent still belongs to the humans.

What this does not do, and what is not built yet

  • The room here is synthetic. These are engine agents raised by this server with declared capability strings, not real companies, and no real deal is behind any row.
  • Matching is exact on declared strings. Two sides meet on the strings they share, so the vocabulary you choose is the vocabulary you match on.
  • The other side learns how many features you submitted, not which ones.
  • Online probing of a single guess is not closed. Any set intersection answers for the elements you submit, which is why one exchange per session and a feature cap exist.
  • Nothing is proven about the humans behind the agents. A card proves which key published an agent.
  • Issuing verified statements about other people's features is not implemented, deliberately.
  • No accounts, no payments, no live counterparties yet. This is the working service on real infrastructure; the first real room is the next step, and it needs real participants.
