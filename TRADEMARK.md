The source code and the normative specification text of the ARLI Discovery Extension are
licensed under Apache-2.0, which allows modification, redistribution and commercial use.

That licence covers the text and the code. It does not cover names or marks.

## What is reserved

- The name **ARLI** and the ARLI logo.
- The product names **ARLI Studio**, **ARLI Task**, **ARLINK**.
- The **ARLI verified** conformance mark and any phrase presenting an implementation as
  certified by ARLI.

All of the above remain the property of Shenzhen Capernaum Technology Co., Ltd.

## What this means in practice

- You may implement the specification, ship it, fork it and sell it, under Apache-2.0.
- You may state factually that your implementation passes the ARLI conformance suite.
- You may not present your implementation as an ARLI product, and you may not use the
  conformance mark as your own branding.
- A claim of conformance in public material should cite the suite version and the result,
  for example: "passes the ARLI Discovery conformance suite v1.0.0, 26 of 26 checks".
  A claim without a version and a result is not a claim we can stand behind.

## Certification, if we offer it

Any certification programme is a separate service agreement. It is not granted by the
licence, and the licence does not oblige us to offer it. Read this together with
`spec/PUBLISHING.md`.
