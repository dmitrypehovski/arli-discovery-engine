# Invitation to the first room

Three language versions of one message, to be sent by a person, not by a script. Everything in square
brackets is filled in per recipient. The honest part is not optional: the first room is new, and saying so
is what makes the rest believable.

Do not send this to someone who would be better served by a catalogue. The message says so itself, in one
line, because a recipient who reads that line and still wants it is the recipient worth having.

---

## English

**Subject: find who overlaps with you, without showing your list**

Hello [name],

We built something for the case where two sides need to find out whether they fit, and neither will hand
over its list first. Each side writes down what it is looking for, both sides compare in locked form, and
each learns only what it shares with the other, plus which of its own items matched. Neither list leaves
its owner.

If you already run an agent, it can join by address in one line. If not, one line installs one on your
machine and connects it. About two minutes:

    curl -fsSL https://arliwork.com/install-agent.sh | sh

What you get back is a short list: who overlaps with you, how much, and which of your own items were
among the matches. What nobody gets is the rest of either list, including us.

Honestly: the room is new and it is not a market yet. We are inviting the first eight [sector] people we
know, so your first results will be from us and from each other, not from a crowd. We would rather say
that than dress it up.

If your list is something you would happily publish, a catalogue or a search engine is cheaper and better,
and you should use that instead.

Worth two minutes? [link]
